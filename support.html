A little help with your fruit.

Drag to aim, release to drop, and match identical fruit to grow something new.

You can stack above the dotted guide. A round ends when a whole fruit spills outside the basket or through the circular bowl's opening.

Contact the developer

Tumble Grove is published by Francesco Moramarco. For help, bug reports or general privacy questions, use the game's public issue tracker:

Get support on GitHub Issues

Posting requires a GitHub account and an internet connection. Your username, issue and attachments are public. This is not a private support channel.

For a bug report, include your device model, iOS or browser version, app version, which game mode you were using, and the steps that caused the problem. A screenshot can help if it contains no private information.

Do not post passwords, email addresses, phone numbers, payment details or other personal or sensitive information. Read the privacy policy for how public support posts, local game data and third-party websites are handled.

Common questions

Can I play without internet?

Yes. The installed iOS game includes its fruit artwork and game resources, so it works offline from its first launch. Its bundled privacy, support, credits and fruit-size pages work offline too. Posting a support issue and visiting external links require an internet connection.

For the browser or Home Screen web app, open the game online first and wait for Ready for offline play. Browser storage must remain available for that cached copy to work.

How can I see the name of a larger fruit?

After at least one drop, choose Inspect, then tap the fruit you are curious about. Choose Resume to keep playing. Inspect pauses the board and does not drop another fruit. Newly merged fruit also display their names briefly.

How do I use Gravity mode?

Enable Gravity mode and tilt your device. Allow motion access if asked. Fruit roll around the circular bowl, and its opening and dotted guide follow gravity. Switching game modes starts a new round; an active round asks for confirmation first.

If motion is unavailable, Classic mode still works. Gravity mode needs a device with motion sensors. In the browser version, use the secure game address and a browser that supports device orientation.

What does Fruit powers do?

In the browser game, turn on Fruit powers beside Gravity mode. Matching fruit activate an ability: Gather draws nearby matching pairs together, Zest helps nearby fruit slide, and Choose pauses the basket while you pick the fruit to drop next. Choosing does not drop it. Bigger merges make stronger powers.

The ready fruit and Inspect show its ability. Powers work in both arenas. The switch is remembered on this device; changing it starts a new round after confirmation. Powers and ordinary rounds have separate best scores.

Where did my round go?

A page reload or app-process restart starts a new round. Bringing an app that is still running back to the foreground preserves its current round. Best scores, appearance and fruit history stay on the device when local storage is available.

The App Store app has separate storage from Safari and an installed Home Screen web app, so scores do not transfer between them automatically.

Can I remove my saved data?

Deleting the iOS app removes its local records. Offloading an app keeps its data. Device backups may restore data according to your backup settings. For the browser version, clear Tumble Grove's website data in your browser settings.

How do I change sound or appearance?

Use the sound and moon/sun controls above the game. Sound is optional, and light/dark appearance can change without starting a new round. The game also respects your device or browser's reduced-motion preference.

What if the game cannot open?

If the iOS app shows a recovery screen, choose Restart game to begin a new round. Your saved best scores remain on the device. If the problem continues, contact the developer with your device and app details.
