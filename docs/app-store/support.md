# Tumble Grove support

Tumble Grove is a fruit-stacking puzzle. Drag to aim and release to drop. Match
two identical fruit to grow a larger one. You can stack above the dotted guide;
the round ends when a fruit spills outside.

## Ask for help or report a bug

For a public bug report or suggestion, visit
[Tumble Grove on GitHub](https://github.com/fran-mora/tumble-grove/issues).
Include your device model, iOS version, app version, which mode you were playing
and the steps that led to the problem. A screenshot can help if it contains no
private information. GitHub posts are public and require a GitHub account.

Use the private contact listed on this page for privacy questions or information
that should not be posted publicly. Do not include passwords, payment details
or other sensitive information in a bug report.

## Common questions

**Can I play without internet?** Yes. The installed iOS game contains its fruit
artwork and game resources. External support and research links need internet.

**How do I see the name of a larger fruit?** After at least one drop, choose
Inspect, then tap the fruit. Choose Resume to continue playing. Inspect pauses
the board and does not drop another fruit.

**How do I use Gravity mode?** Enable Gravity mode and tilt your iPhone. Allow
motion access if asked. The circular bowl and dotted guide follow gravity. If
motion is unavailable, classic mode still works.

**Where did my round go?** A fresh app launch starts a new round. Personal bests,
appearance and fruit history stay on the device when storage is available.
Scores from Safari and an installed Home Screen web app are separate from the
App Store app.

**Can I remove saved data?** Deleting the app removes its local records. Offloading
an app keeps its data. Device backups may restore data according to your Apple
backup settings.

**How do I turn off sound or change appearance?** Use the sound and moon/sun
controls above the game. Pausing, Inspect and Help are also available there.
