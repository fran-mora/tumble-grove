## Initial comparison

The App Store's visible promotional screenshots were viewed on 8 September
2026. They show flat, dark-outlined fruit characters, a purple upper background,
and a pale container. The present Tumble Grove build uses shaded clay-like fruit
art, a cream/green interface, and its own title and control layout. Both display
fruit dropping into a container and merging. Tumble Grove's source also includes
a rotating circular bowl, phone tilt, randomly selected fruit varieties and
offline play. These observations describe visible differences; they do not
establish that any difference is legally sufficient or unique to Tumble Grove.

This was a comparison with publicly displayed promotional images, **not** a
playthrough of the original app, an examination of every skin or animation, a
source-code comparison, or an assessment of substantial copying. The listed
game may have changed since the maintainer first played it. The listing itself
also describes spills; do not claim that spill-based game-over is exclusive to
Tumble Grove. No original-game screenshots or assets were added to this repo.
