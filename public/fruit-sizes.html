Fruit sizes & levels.

All 110 fruit characters, ranked by typical whole-fruit weight and divided into 11 levels of exactly 10. Each game randomly chooses one character from each level.

These are representative sizes, not universal averages. 60 entries use study averages; 50 use published typical weights, range estimates, market grades or proxies. A study average applies to its sampled varieties and growing conditions. A range midpoint is an estimate, not a measured mean.

We compare one whole fruit, including skin and seeds—not a serving or a bunch. Cluster artwork (such as grapes and paired cherries) is decorative. Coconut includes the husk; pineapple data can include the crown. Colour-only names use an identified representative or a labelled proxy.

Values are rounded to three significant figures, sorted from lightest to heaviest, then grouped in consecutive tens. Equal values use the original artwork ID as a stable tie-breaker and can fall on either side of a level boundary. Weight is a practical proxy for size; density, shape and natural size ranges vary and overlap. On-screen body area grows by level, with compressed sizes for playability, rather than literal real-world scale.
